05Fit

Who this is for — and who it isn't.

A small, focused system works when the business process is simple enough to describe. If it isn't, we should say so early.

+ Good fit

  • Small manufacturers, job shops and traders with 20–500 products
  • Owner still checks stock, orders or month-end numbers personally
  • Data already exists in Excel, notebooks or WhatsApp — just not together
  • You can spare 2–3 hours across the week for setup and feedback
  • You want one useful system now, not a two-year ERP rollout

– Not a fit yet

  • You need statutory accounting, GST filing or payroll compliance
  • You need machine-level IoT, SCADA or production automation
  • Your process changes every week and cannot be written down
  • You need a 50-user ERP with approvals, costing and multi-plant logic
  • You are looking for the cheapest possible template with no scoping

07Questions

Before you book a call.

If something is missing, ask directly on WhatsApp. A short answer is better than a long brochure.

Do I need to install any software?
Usually no. The system runs in a browser and is shared as a link. If you choose a Google Sheets build, you need a Google account — which is free. Nothing is installed on your factory computers unless we agree on it in the scope.
Who owns the data and the account?
You do. The Google account, spreadsheet or hosting is created in your name, and you keep admin access. Zeroth is the developer, not the owner of your business data. We do not use one customer's data in another customer's demo.
What does "one revision round" mean?
Within 14 days of delivery, you can send one consolidated list of changes that fall inside the agreed scope — labels, fields, calculations, layout. New modules or changed requirements are quoted separately before any work starts.
Will it work on my phone?
The dashboards and registers are responsive, so you can check stock or sales from a phone. Data entry is faster on a laptop, and that is what we recommend for purchases and sales entry.
Why is the price so low?
Three reasons: the first systems reuse a proven template, delivery is fully remote, and these are introductory prices while we build a public portfolio. The price will rise as case studies and repeatable modules replace the pilot phase.
Do you visit the factory?
No. The entire process is remote: Google Meet for the demo, WhatsApp for questions, and a shared screen for training. This keeps the price low and the delivery fast. If a visit is ever necessary, it is quoted separately and agreed in advance.
What do you need from me to start?
A product list (even a rough Excel), one sample purchase bill, one sample sales invoice, and the name of the report you currently prepare by hand. That is enough for a first working version.
